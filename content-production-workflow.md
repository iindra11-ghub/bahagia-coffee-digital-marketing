# Content Production Workflow
## Bahagia Coffee And Street

### 1. Workflow Overview

The content production process follows a trend-based research and owner-approval workflow.

### 2. Content Research Process

**Step 1: Trend Identification**

Identify trending video formats and content ideas across social media platforms.

**Step 2: Trend Relevance**

Observe trends published or recreated within the same relevant date or month to identify current content opportunities.

**Step 3: Hook Analysis**

Analyze how creators capture audience attention through opening scenes, text, dialogue, and visual elements.

**Step 4: Content Selection**

Select relevant video references and organize them into a content idea list.

**Step 5: Owner Approval**

Share selected video references with the business owner for review and approval.

**Step 6: Content Production**

Produce the approved concept through video recording and editing.

**Step 7: Publishing and Evaluation**

Publish the finished content and evaluate its performance using available social media metrics.

### 3. Key Considerations

Each content reference should be evaluated based on:

- Trend relevance
- Opening hook
- Visual concept
- Brand suitability
- Product relevance
- Production feasibility

### 4. Conclusion

This workflow combines trend research, creative analysis, owner approval, and content production to support the social media marketing activities of Bahagia Coffee And Street.
