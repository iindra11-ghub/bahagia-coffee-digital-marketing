# Content Strategy
## Bahagia Coffee And Street

### 1. Content Strategy Overview

This document explains the creative concepts, storytelling approaches, and marketing objectives behind two Instagram Reels videos produced for Bahagia Coffee And Street.

The content focuses on introducing beverage products, increasing brand awareness, and presenting the coffee shop through engaging visual content.

---

### 2. Video 1: Bikin Minuman Sesuai Mood Barista

**Content Type:** Storytelling and Product Showcase

**Main Objective:** Introduce the Sky Blue beverage while creating an entertaining connection with the audience.

#### Video Flow

1. Opening: The barista greets the audience and introduces the idea of making a drink based on the barista's mood.
2. Entertainment: The barista performs a short freestyle during the preparation process.
3. Product Preparation: The video shows the process of making Sky Blue.
4. Product Showcase: The finished Sky Blue drink is presented in front of the camera.
5. Closing: The Bahagia Coffee And Street logo appears as a pop-up animation.

#### Marketing Analysis

- Uses a human-centered opening through direct interaction with the barista.
- Combines entertainment and beverage preparation.
- Highlights the product through a visual presentation.
- Reinforces brand identity through the closing logo.

---

### 3. Video 2: Hari Ini Mood Kamu Yang Mana?

**Content Type:** Visual Transition and Product Showcase

**Main Objective:** Introduce several beverage options and help audiences discover the available menu.

#### Video Flow

1. Opening: The barista walks naturally toward the camera.
2. Visual Setup: The barista approaches a drink positioned in front of the camera.
3. Transition: Moving the drink from left to right creates a visual transition between different beverage options.
4. Product Showcase: Three beverages are presented:
   - Butterchoot
   - Sky Blue
   - Bahagia Coffee Aren
5. Menu Information: Additional text introduces other menu categories, including Best Coffee and Non Coffee.
6. Closing: The Bahagia Coffee And Street logo appears as a pop-up animation.

#### Marketing Analysis

- Uses visual movement to attract initial attention.
- Presents multiple beverage options in one video.
- Helps audiences discover the coffee shop's menu variety.
- Uses text overlays to communicate additional product categories.
- Reinforces brand recognition through consistent logo placement.

---

### 4. Comparison Between Both Videos

| Aspect | Video 1 | Video 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Main Approach | Storytelling | Visual transition |
| Main Focus | One beverage | Multiple beverages |

### 5. Initial Strategic Insights

Both videos use different creative approaches to introduce Bahagia Coffee And Street.

Video 1 emphasizes the barista's personality and the beverage preparation process, while Video 2 emphasizes menu variety through visual transitions.

These approaches provide different opportunities for evaluating audience responses.

### 6. Future Improvements

- Test different opening hooks.
- Experiment with direct audience interaction.
- Add a clear call-to-action (CTA).
- Evaluate audience retention through average watch time.
- Compare performance using equal measurement periods.

### 7. Conclusion

The two videos represent an initial exploration of short-form video content strategies for Bahagia Coffee And Street.

Further content development will be guided by performance data and audience behavior.
