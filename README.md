# Bahagia Coffee And Street
## Real-World Digital Marketing Case Study

A practical digital marketing project focused on social media auditing, content strategy, short-form video production, and performance analysis for Bahagia Coffee And Street, a local coffee shop in the Madiun area.

## 1. Project Overview

This project demonstrates the practical application of digital marketing concepts in a real-world small business environment.

The project focuses on evaluating social media activity, researching content opportunities, developing marketing strategies, producing promotional videos, and analyzing available performance data.

## 2. Project Background

Bahagia Coffee And Street had an inactive social media account for an extended period before content production began.

As an initial effort to rebuild its online presence, two Instagram Reels videos were produced and published.

This project uses the experience as a foundation for evaluating content performance and identifying future marketing opportunities.

## 3. Project Objectives

- Evaluate existing social media activity.
- Develop relevant content strategies.
- Research current content trends.
- Produce engaging short-form videos.
- Monitor and analyze content performance.
- Explore local SEO and GEO opportunities.
- Develop data-informed marketing recommendations.
