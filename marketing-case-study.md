# Marketing Case Study
## Bahagia Coffee And Street

### 1. Project Overview

Bahagia Coffee And Street is a local coffee shop located around the Madiun area.

This project focuses on applying digital marketing concepts to a real-world small business through social media research, content strategy, video production, and performance evaluation.

**Project Type:** Real-World Digital Marketing Project  
**Industry:** Food & Beverage  
**Marketing Channel:** Instagram  
**Project Status:** Ongoing

### 2. Marketing Opportunities

During the initial social media audit, several opportunities were identified:

- Improving content consistency.
- Developing stronger video hooks.
- Showcasing the coffee shop's atmosphere and products.
- Increasing audience interaction.
- Improving local audience discovery.

These opportunities became the foundation for the proposed content strategy.

### 3. Marketing Strategy

The project applies several digital marketing approaches:

**A. Social Media Audit**

Review existing social media content to identify potential improvements.

**B. Trend-Based Content Research**

Research relevant content trends, analyze creative approaches, and identify suitable references.

**C. Content Strategy**

Develop content ideas based on product showcases, customer experiences, promotional opportunities, and behind-the-scenes activities.

**D. Local SEO and GEO**

Explore relevant local keywords to improve business discoverability across search engines and AI-powered platforms.

### 4. Content Production

Two Instagram Reels videos were produced as part of the project.

The production process included:

1. Content research.
2. Reference selection.
3. Concept development.
4. Video recording and editing.
5. Content publishing.
6. Performance monitoring.

Content production was focused on creating promotional material suitable for the business.

### 5. Performance Analysis

The following data was recorded from two published videos:

| Metric | Video 1 | Video 2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Views | 2,412 | 611 |
| Comments | 2 | 0 |

**Initial Observations:**

- Video 1 generated more views than Video 2.
- Video 1 received two comments.
- Video 2 did not receive any comments.

The available data provides an initial comparison of content performance.

However, additional metrics such as reach, watch time, shares, and profile visits are needed for a more comprehensive evaluation.

### 6. Key Learnings

This project provided practical experience in:

- Conducting social media audits.
- Researching content trends.
- Developing content strategies.
- Producing promotional videos.
- Monitoring social media performance.
- Identifying opportunities for local digital marketing.

It also demonstrated the importance of evaluating content using actual performance data rather than assumptions.

### 7. Future Improvements

The next development phase will focus on:

- Testing different video hooks.
- Developing more consistent content formats.
- Improving audience interaction.
- Exploring local SEO and GEO opportunities.
- Collecting more complete performance metrics.
- Comparing results across multiple content publications.

### 8. Conclusion

The Bahagia Coffee And Street project demonstrates the application of digital marketing concepts in a real-world small business environment.

Through research, strategy development, content production, and performance analysis, this project serves as practical experience in developing data-informed digital marketing activities.
