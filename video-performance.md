# Video Performance Analysis
## Bahagia Coffee And Street

### 1. Overview

This document evaluates the initial performance of two Instagram Reels videos produced for Bahagia Coffee And Street.

### 2. Performance Data

| Metrics | Video 1 | Video 2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Content Title | Bikin minuman sesuai mood barista | Hari ini mood kamu yg mana? |
| Views | 2,412 | 611 |
| Likes | 52 | 21 |
| Comments | 2 | 0 |
| Shares | 2 | 1 |
| Engagement Rate | 2.32% | 3.60% |

### 3. Publishing Timeline

| Video | Upload Age | Upload Interval |
|---|---|---|
| Video 1 | 3 days | - |
| Video 2 | 1 day | 2 days after Video 1 |

The two videos were published with a two-day interval. Since they have different publication ages, their total views should not be compared as an equal-duration performance test.

### 4. Initial Analysis

**Video 1**
- Generated 2,412 views.
- Received 52 likes, 2 comments, and 2 shares.
- Achieved higher overall reach in terms of views compared to Video 2.

**Video 2**
- Generated 611 views.
- Received 21 likes, 0 comments, and 1 share.
- Achieved a higher engagement rate based on recorded interactions.

### 5. Preliminary Findings

Video 1 generated more views and total interactions.

However, Video 2 achieved a higher engagement rate relative to its views.

Further analysis is required to understand audience retention and content effectiveness.

These results provide an initial baseline for evaluating short-form video content performance.

Future content decisions will be informed by additional performance data and audience behavior.
