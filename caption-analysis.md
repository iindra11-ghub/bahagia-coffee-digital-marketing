# Caption Analysis
## Bahagia Coffee And Street

### 1. Overview

This document analyzes the copywriting and call-to-action (CTA) strategies used in two Instagram Reels captions.

The analysis focuses on audience engagement, local brand awareness, and product discovery.

### 2. Video 1: Bikin Minuman Sesuai Mood Barista

**Opening Hook:** "Mood kamu hari ini warna apa?"

**Caption Strategy:** Engagement and Local Awareness

**Key Elements:**
- Uses an open-ended question to attract audience attention.
- Connects the audience's mood with the Sky Blue beverage.
- Includes the business location in Wungu, Madiun.
- Provides operating hours and closing-day information.
- Uses local hashtags to improve content relevance.

**CTA Analysis:**

The caption encourages audiences to think about their mood and introduces the coffee shop as a local destination.

However, the opening question does not explicitly instruct audiences to leave a comment.

### 3. Video 2: Hari Ini Mood Kamu Yang Mana?

**Opening Hook:** "Hari ini mood kamu yang mana?"

**Caption Strategy:** Engagement and Product Discovery

**Key Elements:**
- Introduces several beverage choices.
- Groups menu options into numbered selections.
- Encourages audiences to participate through a simple comment-based CTA.
- Uses local hashtags to support geographic relevance.

**CTA:** "Kalau mau cerita pilihanmu, tulis angkanya di komen."

**CTA Analysis:**

The caption provides a specific and relatively simple action for audiences: choosing a number and commenting.

This approach reduces the effort required to participate in the conversation.

### 4. Caption Comparison

| Aspect | Video 1 | Video 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Objective | Local Awareness | Product Discovery |
| Hook Type | Open-ended Question | Choice-based Question |
| CTA | General engagement and visit invitation | Comment a number |
| Location Information | Available | Not included |
| Interaction Direction | Less specific | More specific |

### 5. Preliminary Findings

Video 2 uses a more explicit engagement CTA than Video 1.

However, the available performance data does not yet demonstrate that the second CTA generated more comments.

Further testing is necessary before drawing conclusions about CTA effectiveness.

### 6. Recommendations

- Test more specific comment-based CTAs.
- Experiment with questions related to customer preferences.
- Maintain relevant local information in promotional captions.
- Evaluate comments and profile visits alongside video views.
- Keep caption content consistent with the products shown in the video.

### 7. Conclusion

Both captions use mood-based questions to connect beverage products with audience preferences.

The main difference is the CTA approach: Video 1 combines engagement with local business information, while Video 2 encourages direct audience participation.
