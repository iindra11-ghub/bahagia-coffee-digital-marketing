# Strategic Recommendations
## Bahagia Coffee And Street

### 1. Project Objective

The purpose of these strategic recommendations is to identify opportunities to improve Bahagia Coffee And Street's social media marketing performance through content optimization, audience engagement, and local digital visibility.

### 2. Content Strategy Recommendations

**A. Strengthen Content Hooks**

Develop stronger opening scenes and messages to capture audience attention within the first few seconds.

Suggested content angles:
- Is this a comfortable place to study?
- Looking for an affordable coffee spot around Madiun?
- A closer look at the atmosphere of Bahagia Coffee.

**B. Develop Consistent Content Pillars**

Focus on several content categories:

- Product Showcase: Highlight coffee, food, and other menu options.
- Place Experience: Showcase the atmosphere, seating areas, and customer experience.
- Promotional Content: Communicate available offers and product value.
- Behind the Scenes: Show coffee preparation and daily activities.

**C. Increase Audience Interaction**

Encourage viewers to interact through:
- Questions in video captions.
- Interactive content about menu preferences.
- Polls and audience suggestions.
- Clear calls to action.

### 3. Local Digital Marketing Recommendations

Improve local discoverability by incorporating relevant location-based keywords into social media captions.

Suggested keyword examples:
- Coffee shop Madiun
- Tempat nongkrong Madiun
- Cafe untuk belajar di Madiun
- Coffee shop Kabupaten Madiun

Use consistent location information and relevant hashtags to support local audience discovery.

### 4. Performance Optimization

Evaluate each published video using available metrics:

- Views: Measure content reach.
- Likes: Understand audience reactions.
- Comments: Measure audience interaction.
- Shares: Identify content with sharing potential.
- Profile Visits: Observe interest in the business profile.

Compare content performance to identify which hooks, topics, and formats generate better audience responses.

### 5. Implementation Priorities

| Priority | Recommended Action |
|---|---|
| High | Improve video hooks |
| High | Develop consistent content pillars |
| Medium | Increase audience interaction |
| Medium | Optimize local keywords |
| Medium | Evaluate content performance |

### 6. Expected Outcomes

The recommendations are intended to:

- Improve content consistency.
- Strengthen brand awareness.
- Increase audience engagement opportunities.
- Improve local digital visibility.
- Support data-driven content decisions.

These outcomes are objectives, not guaranteed results.

### 7. Conclusion

By combining creative content development, local digital marketing, and performance evaluation, Bahagia Coffee And Street can develop a more structured social media marketing strategy.
