# Social Media Audit
## Bahagia Coffee And Street

### 1. Audit Overview

This audit evaluates the initial social media condition of Bahagia Coffee And Street before and during the implementation of a short-form video content strategy.

**Platform:** Instagram  
**Content Format:** Instagram Reels  
**Audit Type:** Initial Social Media Audit

### 2. Initial Account Condition

Based on initial observations:

- The account had been inactive for an extended period.
- Consistent content publishing had not been maintained.
- Two new short-form videos have been produced and published as an initial content activation effort.

### 3. Content Observation

| Aspect | Initial Observation |
|---|---|
| Publishing Activity | Previously inactive |
| Content Format | Short-form video / Reels |
| Published Videos | 2 |
| Content Consistency | Needs improvement |
| Audience Response | Requires further analysis |

### 4. Key Issues Identified

1. Inconsistent content publishing activity.
2. Limited recent content activity.
3. Insufficient performance data to evaluate audience response.
4. The effectiveness of the new content strategy has not yet been established.

### 5. Improvement Opportunities

- Establish a consistent content schedule.
- Develop engaging video hooks.
- Highlight products, prices, and coffee shop atmosphere.
- Monitor views, reach, interactions, and profile visits.
- Evaluate content performance before making strategic adjustments.

### 6. Audit Conclusion

The initial audit indicates an opportunity to improve the business's social media presence through consistent content publishing and performance-based evaluation.

Further analysis will be conducted as more content performance data becomes available.
